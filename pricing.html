How Pro works

No account to create and no password. Your licence key is your sign-in.

  1. Choose a planPress Get Pro here or Upgrade inside the extension. A secure checkout by Freemius opens. Pay by card or PayPal.
  2. Get your licence keyIt shows on the thank-you screen right away and is emailed to the address you used at checkout.
  3. Activate in the extensionOpen Pro, paste your key and press Activate. You'll see "Signed in as" your email, your plan and its renewal date.
  4. Manage any timeUse Manage account to see invoices, cancel or find your key again. Sign out on one device to use Pro on another.

Payments and licence keys for Pro are handled by our reseller and merchant of record, Freemius, Inc. Taxes may be added at checkout depending on where you live. See the Terms of Service. Statutory consumer rights, where they apply, are not affected.
