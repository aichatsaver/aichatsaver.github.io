For Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge.

Works with 18 AIs

Sign in to the AIs you use in your browser as usual. {{ s.product_name }} saves the chats from each one into its own folder.

Everything in the chat, not just the text

Your chats become real files you own: readable offline, searchable, and ready to export.

A local folder you own

Chats are written to Downloads/AI Chat Saver, one folder per AI, as Markdown, HTML and JSON, with media alongside. Or connect a folder once and files save straight into it, with no Save-As boxes.

One-click incremental Update

One Update button syncs every AI and saves only chats that are new or changed, so nothing is downloaded twice. Run it from the popup, press Alt+Shift+U, or schedule it hourly, every 6 hours, daily or weekly.

Media, thinking, code and artifacts kept

  • Uploaded and generated images, videos and files
  • Thinking and reasoning, tool calls, code the AI ran and its output
  • Web sources and citations
  • Claude artifacts and ChatGPT canvases as real files
  • Messages deleted on the site stay, marked “Deleted on site”; edited prompts are kept as versions

Exports that look like the original

Export to HTML, Markdown, PDF, Word (DOCX) and TXT, plus JSON, PNG and JPG. Each export can keep the AI site’s own look, in light or dark. PDFs are made on your computer with real, selectable text, with no print dialog.

Search and the Command Center

Press {{ s.command_center_shortcut }} to open the full-screen Command Center: instant full-text search across every AI, filters like provider: and has:code, a command palette (Ctrl+K), a Timeline, highlights and notes, and Reading mode.

Private by design

Everything runs in your browser using your own signed-in sessions. Your chats stay on your device: no account with us, no servers, no analytics.

How it works

  1. Install the extensionAdd it to Chrome or Edge and pin the icon.
  2. Stay signed in to your AIsUse the AI sites in the same browser profile, as you already do.
  3. Press UpdateYour chats are saved to your folder. Press it again any time; only new and changed chats are saved.

Privacy

Your chats never come to us

{{ s.product_name }} reads your chats through your browser from the AI sites you are signed in to, and writes them only to your own folder and your browser’s local storage. Read the full Privacy Policy.

  • No chat content is sent to the developer
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No selling of data, ever
  • Uninstall or delete the folder to remove everything

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. {{ s.product_name }} uses the sessions you already have on each AI site. Pro will use a licence key handled by our payment partner, Freemius.

Where are my chats saved?

In Downloads/AI Chat Saver on your computer (one folder per AI), or in a folder you connect. A copy is also kept in your browser’s local storage so search is instant.

What happens if an AI site changes?

After each sync the extension checks every AI. If a site changed and saving stops working, you get one notification and a banner, and a broken run never overwrites a good saved chat.
