Quick links

Drafts

Use Add to drafts in any section to collect changes here, then publish them together as one change. Drafts are kept in this browser until you publish or discard them.

Homepage

Texts are saved to the language’s translation file; section order, visibility and extra hero buttons to _data/home.yml. Simple HTML such as <strong> and <kbd> is allowed. %name% becomes the product name.

Preview

Pricing

Prices, currency and feature lists are saved to _data/site.yml; launch, checkout links, highlight and the Lifetime card to _data/pricing.yml; plan names and button texts to the language’s translation file. Plan order is fixed: Free, Pro Yearly, Pro Monthly, then Lifetime when it’s on.

Preview

FAQ

Each language has its own list. In answers you can use %name%, %email%, %pro_status%, %pricing_url% and simple HTML links.

Preview

Supported AIs

The logo tiles on the homepage, saved to _data/ais.yml. The name shows when a visitor hovers a logo. Uploaded logos go to assets/images/uploads/.

Preview

Navigation & footer

Saved to _data/navigation.yml. Built-in labels are translated automatically; your own labels show as typed. The Refund Policy stays out of the top bar, and the admin panel can never be linked.

Preview

Coupon / Deal

Type a coupon you created in Freemius (Marketing → Coupons). While it’s on and between the start and end, every buy button opens the Freemius checkout with the coupon already applied. Saved to _data/coupon.yml. Off by default.

Preview

Announcement bar

A slim bar at the top of every public page, saved to _data/announcement.yml. It shows only while it’s switched on.

Preview

Store links & launch

Saved to _data/site.yml. Leave a link empty to show a “Coming soon” badge. The version shows under the store buttons on the homepage.

Preview

SEO & sharing

Saved to _data/seo.yml. Leave a field empty to keep the automatic value (shown greyed out). Titles show best under 60 characters, descriptions under 160.

Languages

Switching a language off hides it from the language menu, search engines and the sitemap, and its pages stop being built. English is the main language and is always on.

Maintenance mode

When on, every public page shows a short “back soon” page instead of the site. This admin panel keeps working. Saved to _data/maintenance.yml.

Preview

Site settings

Saved to _data/site.yml. Prices: write TBD (or leave empty) to show “Coming soon”. Store links: leave empty to show a “Coming soon” badge. English pages always show these values. If you change the tagline, description, Pro status or feature lists, the other languages show your new English text until you update that language under Translations (the meta keys).

Policies

Saved to pages/privacy.md. Policies are in English only. Text like {{ s.product_name }} is filled in from Site settings when the site builds. Remember to update “Policies updated” in Site settings after a real change.

Preview

Translations

Saved to _data/i18n/es.yml. Keep placeholders such as %name%, %email%, %shortcut% and any HTML tags exactly as in English.

Media library

Images in assets/images/uploads/. PNG, JPG, SVG or WebP, up to 1 MB each. SVG files with scripts or outside links are refused. An image can be deleted only when no page or setting uses it.
